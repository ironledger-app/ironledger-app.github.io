Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Iron Ledger is run by Gábor Domsitz, Slovakia (publishing as Iron Ledger Apps). Contact: GDomsitz@gmail.com.

What Iron Ledger stores

  • Account: your email address.
  • Training data you enter: programs, workouts, sets, reps and weights, your feedback notes, your daily check-in (sleep, energy, soreness), equipment and training space, bodyweight, and injury notes.
  • Health Connect data, only if you connect it: last night’s sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and weight. Iron Ledger reads these to judge your recovery. It writes your finished workouts to Health Connect as exercise sessions.
  • AI coach usage: the time and size of each coach request, to apply usage limits and keep costs under control.

How it is used

Your data is used only to run the app for you: to save your training, show your progress, and let the AI coach plan and adjust your workouts. To do that, the relevant parts of your training data, check-in and Health Connect readings are sent to Anthropic’s Claude API each time the coach writes or adjusts a plan. Anthropic processes them to produce the answer and does not use them to train its models.

Iron Ledger does not sell your data, does not show ads and does not use your data for advertising.

Health Connect

Iron Ledger’s use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Health Connect data is used only for the features described above, is not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide those features (the AI coach), and is never used for advertising. You can remove the permissions at any time in Health Connect.

Strava

If you connect Strava, Iron Ledger uploads your finished workouts to your Strava account. It does not read any data from Strava. You can disconnect in the app at any time, which also revokes Iron Ledger’s access.

Where data is kept

Your account and training data are stored with Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). Data is encrypted in transit. A copy is kept on your phone so the app works offline.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account and all data. This permanently removes your account, programs and training log from our servers. You can also ask for deletion by email. See Delete your account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted. Write to GDomsitz@gmail.com. You can also complain to your national data protection authority; in Slovakia this is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR).

Children

Iron Ledger is not meant for children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
