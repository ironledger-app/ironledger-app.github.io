Iron Ledger

A strength-training planner and tracker for kettlebells, barbells and bodyweight. Plan your programs, log every set, and let an AI coach adjust your training to how you actually perform.

Coming soon to Google Play
Plan

Run several programs at once: Simple & Sinister, 5/3/1, ladders, custom plans. Exact weight, reps and rest for every session.

Track

Log planned vs actual sets, rest timer, hands-free follow-along for circuits and intervals, progress charts.

Adapt

The AI coach drafts and adjusts plans from your logs, check-ins and equipment. Nothing changes until you approve it.

Your data

Your training data is stored in the EU, never sold and never used for ads. Read the privacy policy and the terms of service. You can delete your account at any time.
