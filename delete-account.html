Delete your account

Iron Ledger, by Iron Ledger Apps (Gábor Domsitz)

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Iron Ledger and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and all data and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away.

Option 2: by email

If you can't use the app any more, send an email to GDomsitz@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Iron Ledger account”. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in details)
  • All programs, workout logs, check-ins, notes, equipment settings and coach chats
  • AI coach usage records
  • Your Strava connection (access is revoked with Strava)

What is kept

  • Nothing on our servers. Copies may remain in encrypted server backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
  • Workouts already written to Health Connect or uploaded to Strava stay there; remove them in those apps if you want.
  • Subscription and payment records are kept by Google Play under Google's own policies.
Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Use one of the options above.
